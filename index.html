Tic-Tac-Toe

Player X’s turn

Score

  • Player X wins: 0
  • Player O wins: 0
  • Draws: 0

How to play

Take turns placing X and O. Get three in a row, column, or diagonal to win.

  • Mouse or touch: select an empty square.
  • Keyboard: press Tab to reach the board, then use the arrow keys to move between squares, and press Enter or Space to place your mark.
  • Number keys 1–9: jump to a square (1 is top-left, 9 is bottom-right).
  • N: new round. R: reset the score. B: hear the whole board read aloud.
